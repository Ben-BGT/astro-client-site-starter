About

A one-person studio with one idea.

Most small business sites are slow, hard to edit, and locked into someone else's platform. I build the opposite.

See my services Get in touch

I've been building websites for small businesses for over a decade. Along the way I've watched clients get trapped in fragile WordPress setups, expensive SaaS CMSes, and page builders that fell out of fashion six months after launch.

This studio is my answer to that pattern. Every site I ship is a plain git repo, a friendly CMS, and a static build that loads in under a second. The copy lives in markdown, the images live in a folder, and nothing is locked behind a vendor.

If you're a founder, an operator, or a marketer who has been burned by the usual website stack, I'd love to hear from you.
